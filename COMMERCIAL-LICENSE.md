Under the AGPL you are free to use, study, modify, and redistribute Nybble,
including inside your own organization, at no cost. In exchange, the AGPL

available to others over a network — you make the complete corresponding source
of your version available to those users under the same license.

For the overwhelming majority of users this is the right option and costs
nothing:

- Reverse engineers, analysts, researchers, and hobbyists using the app
- Teams using the app internally on their own files
- Anyone forking the engine, writing format packs, or contributing back

Nothing about the AGPL restricts the *files* you analyze, or the format packs
and schemas you write. Those are your data and your work, not derivatives of
Nybble.

## Option 2 — Commercial license (paid)

A separate commercial license is available for cases where the AGPL's reciprocal
obligations are not workable. You likely want this if you intend to:

- Embed Nybble, or any of its crates, in a closed-source product you distribute
- Offer Nybble's functionality as part of a hosted or network-accessible service
  without publishing your service's source
- Ship a modified Nybble to customers without releasing your modifications
- Obtain warranty, indemnity, or support terms that the AGPL expressly disclaims

The commercial license grants the same software under negotiated terms, without
the copyleft requirements of section 13 and section 5 of the AGPL.
